JNV
JNV stands for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, a highly successful system of central government residential schools in India designed to find and nurture talented children, predominantly from rural areas. Managed by an autonomous body under the Ministry of Education called the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), these co-educational institutions provide free quality education, boarding, and lodging from Class VI to XII
Nagaur
Nagaur is an ancient historical city and district headquarters in central Rajasthan, India, located between Jodhpur and Bikaner. Spanning 17,718 square kilometers, it is the fifth-largest district in the state and is renowned for the historic Nagaur Fort, vibrant cattle fairs, and its deep-rooted cultural heritage
